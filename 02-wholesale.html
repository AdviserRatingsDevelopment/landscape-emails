The retail-to-wholesale shift, quantified

Now in its 8th edition, Adviser Ratings’ Australian Financial Advice Landscape is no longer static. It’s live on Nightingale, released chapter by chapter, with the data beginning to move as the market does. Chapter 1 lands on a shift you already feel: high-value clients are leaving the retail channel.

The clients exiting retail advice carry an average portfolio north of $1.4M. Around 40% of top-decile relationships rotated out in twelve months, many heading to wholesale, multi-family-office, family office and private wealth. The data behind the migration is now open.
